Three bytes make a colour

A colour code is three bytes in hex: how much red light, then green, then blue. 00 is none of it. FF is 255, all of it. Move the sliders and watch the code.

#FF0000

Light adds up: red and green light together make yellow, and all three at FF make white.

The colour wheel

Tap any point on the wheel. Round the edge, at least one pair is FF and at least one is 00. Towards the middle, all three pairs rise towards FF, which is white.
